Complete Every IXL Link

80+ Required SmartScore for Mastery

Opening Your Assigned Skills

Use the IXL links at the bottom of the page.

  1. Scroll to the bottom of the Practice It! page.
  2. Click each assigned IXL skill link.
  3. Confirm that the link opens your logged-in PPOA IXL account.
  4. Complete the skill until your SmartScore is 80 or higher.

Before You Begin the Skill

Make sure you see your PPOA IXL account. Do not complete the skill while signed out or using a personal IXL account.

Use IXL Feedback Productively

Do not guess repeatedly just to reach 80.

1. Read Study the explanation after an incorrect answer.
2. Correct Identify the step or idea that caused the mistake.
3. Retry Apply the correction to the next problem.
Your goal is mastery, not fast guessing. Slow down, use the feedback, and return to your Learn It notes when needed.
