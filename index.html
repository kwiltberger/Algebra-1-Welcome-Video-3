Use Feedback Productively

Do not guess repeatedly just to finish.

1. Read Study the feedback after an incorrect answer.
2. Correct Identify the step or idea that caused the mistake.
3. Retry Use the correction on the next problem.
A lower score is not a reason to rush. Slow down, review your notes, and use the feedback to improve.
